> I am human, born to build the world from cosmic dust, and shape the world in ways I please.

I was born on planet Earth, wanting to build a better world that inspires generations to come.

I am great at seeing worlds where no poor, weak, sick, or dead exist — and I put my life in the service of making our world as the ones I see.

The strong win and live, while the weak perish. Why not have a world with no weak — making all of our race stronger and wiser.

I am in a state of learning and building, all days and all of the nights. I do not pursue happiness — I only see results.

I sit in the late nights thinking of the infinite possibilities of how our world could become, but also the ways it could go wrong. I do not intend to celebrate the wins — I only build more and more.

My biggest fear is that I may taste pleasure and happiness, and for that forget the pains that killed me — therefore I stop fighting to make sure no one else ever tastes the same pain.
