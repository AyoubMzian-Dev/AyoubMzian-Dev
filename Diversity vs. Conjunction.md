# Introduction

This blog discusses why conjunction and having fewer sources is better than having many sources that provide the same thing with different key features. While I will use educational content as an example, this concept applies broadly across business, software development, and any field where multiple solutions address the same fundamental problem.

# The Central Argument 
The point I am trying to prove is straightforward: instead of fragmenting our efforts across numerous mediocre alternatives, we should consolidate our expertise and resources to create fewer, superior solutions that incorporate the best elements from all approaches.


## The Pattern of Fragmentation

Consider educational YouTube channels that teach science. In reality, we have very few high-quality channels in this space, not because creators have read arguments like this one, but because it represents challenging business territory for those primarily motivated by profit rather than educational impact.

However, when multiple creators do enter this space, we gain the supposed benefit of diversity and choice, but this comes with significant downsides. Each channel develops its own philosophy about effective science education. One creator might believe that high-quality visuals represent the key to understanding, investing heavily in animations, shapes, and visual analogies to convey concepts quickly and clearly. Another might focus on scriptwriting, believing that the right words, analogies, and timing create deeper comprehension.

Each channel has its own vision of what makes the critical difference in achieving the ultimate goal of helping people understand complex subjects without losing interest. Yet each creator must produce the entire package independently. Since visual design or scriptwriting may not represent their primary Idea, they often underinvest in areas outside their core competency. The visually-focused creator may not provide the most engaging scripts or cover topics with sufficient depth, while the script-focused creator may neglect video editing that would enhance viewer engagement and learning retention.

## The Conjunction Alternative

What if these creators collaborated instead of competing? Each contributor could focus on their area of expertise while others handled complementary aspects, each one putting the thing that they believe in the most. The result would be content that combines high-quality visuals with exceptional scripting and professional editing in this case. This approach maximizes each person's contribution while ensuring comprehensive coverage of all elements necessary for effective educational content.

This collaborative model allows everyone to focus on what they do best, in their main idea, while contributing to a superior final product that exceeds what any individual creator could produce alone.

But keeping in mind that this approach may not be the best for some cases, like
personalised products, or solutions that solve the issue in different ways.

## Implementation Challenges

This approach faces significant obstacles that prevent natural occurrence in most industries. The challenges resemble typical team dynamics but with important differences. When each collaborator has their own vision and believes strongly in their particular approach, you essentially create a team where everyone has leadership tendencies and strong convictions about methodology.

This dynamic can produce exceptional results or complete failure, if their visions ever conflicted. The determining factor appears to be flexibility. Unlike traditional teams with clear hierarchical structures, conjunction-based collaboration requires participants who can adapt their approaches while maintaining their expertise focus.

Successful implementation demands deeper relationships between team members, requiring substantial honesty and willingness to compromise for the collective good. This necessitates strong individual confidence and self-awareness, though exploring these psychological requirements extends beyond the scope of this discussion.

## Universal Application

This principle extends far beyond educational content. In software development, instead of having dozens of applications that solve the same problem with varying degrees of effectiveness, consolidating expertise could produce fewer, more comprehensive solutions. In business, rather than multiple companies offering similar products with different strengths and weaknesses, collaborative approaches could yield superior offerings that incorporate the best features from all alternatives.

The pattern repeats across industries: fragmented effort produces multiple mediocre options instead of fewer excellent ones.

## The Results of Conjunction

Implementing this approach would produce several transformative outcomes across industries and society. First, we would see dramatically higher quality content and solutions built upon the collective best ideas of experts in each field. When specialists collaborate rather than compete, their combined expertise creates outputs that exceed what any individual contributor could achieve independently.

Second, this model naturally eliminates space for cheap, profit-driven content that prioritizes quantity over quality. When resources consolidate around excellence, inferior alternatives cannot compete effectively, raising the overall standard for all offerings in the market. This creates an environment where quality becomes the primary differentiator rather than marketing budgets or production speed.

Third, we would achieve significantly better solutions to existing problems across multiple domains. Instead of accepting the limitations inherent in fragmented approaches, conjunction enables comprehensive problem-solving that addresses issues from multiple angles simultaneously. This leads to more robust, effective solutions that consider various perspectives and methodologies.

Fourth, this approach redirects civilization's resources and efforts toward genuine innovation rather than perpetual reinvention of existing solutions. When we stop recreating the wheel repeatedly across different organizations and instead build upon established excellence, human capital becomes available for tackling new challenges and developing breakthrough solutions to previously unsolved problems.

Additionally, conjunction reduces market confusion for consumers who currently must navigate numerous similar options with unclear distinctions. This clarity enables better decision-making and more efficient resource allocation throughout the economy. The approach also creates stronger professional development opportunities, as specialists can focus on advancing their expertise rather than becoming generalists out of competitive necessity.

# Summary

Conjunction represents a more efficient approach than diversity when addressing identical problems. By consolidating expertise and resources rather than fragmenting them across competing alternatives, we can create superior solutions that incorporate the best elements from all approaches. While implementation presents challenges related to collaboration and ego management, the potential benefits justify pursuing this model across educational content, business, software development, and other fields where multiple actors currently duplicate efforts while producing suboptimal individual results.
